### Proposed Solution

We propose to develop a Demand-Aware Canteen Ordering and Real-Time Token Dispatch System that digitises and optimises the existing canteen ordering, billing and food distribution workflow.

The system will provide two ordering methods: advance ordering through a mobile-friendly web application and assisted ordering through the existing billing counter using a digital point-of-sale interface. Students will be able to browse the menu, select food items, make or confirm payments and reserve available pickup slots based on the capacity of the respective food counters.

Once an order is confirmed as paid, the system will automatically generate a unique digital token and dispatch the order to the relevant food counters. Orders containing items from multiple counters will be divided into separate counter-specific tickets while maintaining a single order reference for the student.

Food counter staff will receive orders through dedicated dashboards and update their preparation status in real time. Students will be notified when their food is ready and can collect it by presenting their digital token or QR code, eliminating the need to stand in a second queue merely to submit a printed bill.

The system will also provide an administrative dashboard to monitor order volumes, counter utilisation, peak-hour demand, food popularity and order processing times. Historical order data can be analysed to identify demand patterns and support better food preparation and capacity planning.

The proposed system aims to reduce student waiting times, improve coordination between billing and food distribution counters, and provide data-driven insights for more efficient canteen operations.
